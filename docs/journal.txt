2026-09-01 — fixed-price floor, gate-detail reporting, go live
--------------------------------------------------------------
Resumed monitoring after a session disconnect. The agent had kept running
throughout: poller and auth online under pm2, working tree clean, nothing lost.

Found and closed a hole in screening. At 20:09 a $10 fixed-price GoHighLevel
posting cleared every gate, scored 76.2 — second-highest recorded — and built a
draft that would have spent 8 Connects to bid $9. Only autoApply.live being
false kept it from submitting.

Root cause: scope_fits_budget measures deliverables per $100. Being a ratio, it
can only judge scope relative to budget and cannot floor the budget itself. A
posting with no countable deliverables yields a ratio of zero and passes at any
budget at all. Hourly work already had minHourlyRate; fixed-price had nothing.

Fixed with minFixedBudget ($50), emitted under the existing rate_acceptable
gate name so "the money is too small" stays one rejection reason across both
job types. The check reads the budget alone and never the description, so no
phrasing can buy a tiny budget past it.

That naming choice created a second, smaller problem: with one gate name
covering two job types, a rising counter could not tell you which floor was
doing the work — the new floor was invisible in the status report from the
moment it shipped. Closed by carrying the newest detail string alongside each
count. First run confirmed the floor working in the wild:

    14  rate_acceptable          latest: fixed budget $10, floor $50

Flipped autoApply.live to true at 21:31 UTC on the operator's decision, with
the confirm_draft step still never having executed. Everything upstream is
proven in production; the act of submitting is not.

Also observed, not fixed: a single poller crash at 15:53 on an unguarded read
of connectsBalance from the account payload. One occurrence, self-healed via
backoff, not recurring. It will recur eventually.

2026-09-01 (cont.) — went live, first submission, tone feedback
---------------------------------------------------------------
Flipped autoApply.live to true at 21:31 UTC and restarted the poller. The
restart landed mid-draft on a job that had just cleared every gate — "API
Integration with Trellis Law", 69.5, zero proposals, hourly $15-35 — and killed
the apply in flight. The job was left in `scored`, which nothing sweeps, so it
was stranded within minutes of that exact failure mode being written up in
docs/database.md. Restarting without checking for an in-flight apply is the
mistake; there is no drain and nothing retries.

Recovered it by hand. The re-screen at submit refused it on freshness (14 min
old, window 10), correctly — it was only stale because of the restart. Applied
with UPWORK_AGENT_CONFIG pointed at a scratch config with a wider window, which
leaves the running poller untouched: no restart, no interval where the live
agent might bid on stale postings itself. Every other gate stayed in force.

2026-09-02 — soak check, then Trust & Safety restriction; automation stopped
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Read-only soak of the first 20 hours unattended. Ended with the account
restricted and both processes shut down. No code changed this session.

The run itself was stable: 393 poll cycles at 20/hour from 09-01T14:00, zero
restarts, one error (the 09-01 connectsBalance crash, self-recovered). 196 jobs
seen, 72 screened, 3 submitted and confirmed live on Upwork. 36 connects spent,
balance 130.

Findings, in the order they matter:

  1. The daily cap is first-come, not best-of. Three proposals landed inside 80
     minutes (21:44, 22:56, 23:02 on 09-01) scoring 69.5, 65.4 and 72.8. In the
     22 hours after, six jobs cleared every gate and were refused with "3
     proposals in 24h" — scoring 76.0, 76.4, 81.8, 85.0, 91.4, 91.8. Every
     refused job outranked every submitted one. They sit in `scored`, which
     nothing sweeps; 12 jobs are stranded there.

  2. The drafter names a disqualifier and submits anyway. Both auto-submissions
     went to the same client (8658048), two near-duplicate $50 postings six
     minutes apart, 16 connects. The letters say "I don't operate a marketing or
     lead gen agency" in their own WHERE I DON'T MATCH block. Nothing gates on
     that, and there is no per-client dedupe.

  3. isAuthError misses the real message. poll.ts:47 matches "token expired" and
     "token has expired" but not "token is expired", which is what Upwork sends.
     When authorization was revoked at 10:47 the guard did not fire, no alert
     was raised, and the poller treated a permanent failure as transient and
     backed off. The alerts table has never had a row; the email channel is
     still unproven in production.

  4. Search coverage, traced from one missed posting. A Forward Deployed
     Engineer role (client $249k spend, 4.99/21, $20-40/hr, TypeScript/Claude/
     MCP) never reached the database. Cause is query shape, not gates: adding
     one word the posting lacks evicts it. "Forward Deployed Engineer" ranks it
     first; + "automation" or + "webhook" loses it entirely. The word
     "automation" appears nowhere in that posting, and neither does "webhook" —
     so api-integration and ai-agents, the two profiles built to catch it, each
     miss on their own third word. find_jobs action=smart_search returns it at
     rank 3. The dashboard's matching_jobs feed is a third surface we never read.

  5. Detection latency is bimodal, not a flat 34 min median. Split by whether
     Upwork published a proposal count: omitted (fresh postings) n=104, median
     3.9 min; reported (aged postings) n=96, median 2135 min. The 3-minute loop
     is fast when the surface cooperates. Note our own pool data is censored
     twice — proposals_max 15 hides the tail, and 69 of the recorded zeros are
     inferred rather than observed.

2026-09-04 — letter register: rewrote the compose prompt around genre
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Closes the drafting feedback left open on 09-01 and still listed as unfixed on
09-02. No pipeline ran and nothing contacted Upwork; the account remains stood
down.

The 09-01 note recorded five tics (ALL-CAPS headers, a contrarian stakes-raising
opener, vivid worst-case imagery, aphoristic closers, arrow bullets used for
rhythm) and called the register structural rather than incidental. Regenerating
a letter for a live posting reproduced all five, which confirmed that reading.

The sharper diagnosis is genre, not tone. The old SYSTEM opened "THIS IS A SALES
PITCH", and the model read that as a brief to produce marketing: sectioned,
quotable, a metrics block, an explainer addressed to an audience, a paragraph of
positioning for an adjacent product. That is a capabilities page, not a message
to one buyer. The tics follow from the genre, which is why treating them as
wording would not have held.

Rewrote SYSTEM around what is being written:
  - "A message to one person. Not marketing, not a capabilities page, not a
    LinkedIn post", with an explicit test — if it would work unchanged as a
    landing page it is wrong, and a proposal is one that could only have been
    sent to this client about this post.
  - ANSWER THEIR POST: use their words, engage what they actually wrote.
  - MAKE ONE ARGUMENT: one case, evidence in support of it rather than on
    display, spoken figures rounded rather than a spec-sheet row.
  - Gaps go in a sentence in the flow, not a section of their own.
  - Explicit avoid-list for the five tics, plus a hard rule against em and en
    dashes in any position.
  - Target length 3000-4000 chars -> 1200-1500, on the reasoning that length is
    the clearest genre tell.

Kept verbatim: the evidence-grounding rule, the "Do not claim" handling, the
compliance-marker and injection rules, and the ban on writing that he is the
wrong hire.

Measured on the same posting, same evidence, same model: 4207 chars -> 1681, no
headers, no dashes, opens by quoting the client's own brief, closes on two
questions specific to their setup.

Two things this session did not fix:
  - verify.ts still checks only length, compliance markers and agent-directed
    text. It does not check claims against evidence, and it does not check the
    dash ban. Both are prompt-only, and a prompt instruction is a hope rather
    than a control -- the same lesson the length retry already encodes.
  - The drafting path still cannot be exercised without MCP auth. draft-one.ts
    takes a job id and fetches the posting, so a pasted posting needed a local
    runner in the gitignored data/ to reuse the pipeline.
